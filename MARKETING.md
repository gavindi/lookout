# Lookout

**Your email, calendar, people, and tasks — finally at home on GNOME.**

Some tools ask you to adapt to them. Lookout is built the other way around.

It is a native GNOME app for mail, calendars, contacts and tasks, made for people who want one calm place to get through the day. It keeps the layout and habits you already know from Outlook — folders on the left, messages in the middle, the reading pane on the right, and the actions you use most within easy reach — then fits them into a fast, private desktop app that feels like it belongs on your computer.

No second browser. No new way of thinking. No subscription.

## A calmer way to work

- **Familiar from the first minute.** If you have used Outlook at work — or in a previous life — you already know your way around Lookout. Three panes, a ribbon-style toolbar, conversations, categories, flags and folders all sit where you would expect them.
- **Everything in one window.** A meeting invitation opens right in your mail. A contact's details appear without leaving the message. Your tasks sit beside your schedule instead of in a separate app you forget to open.
- **Quick because it is local.** Folders you have visited paint instantly, messages you have read open instantly, and much of your mail is quietly prepared in the background before you ask for it.
- **Yours to set up, not ours to lock down.** Bring accounts in through GNOME Online Accounts, or add any IMAP/SMTP account yourself. Lookout speaks standard IMAP, SMTP, CalDAV and CardDAV, and keeps your passwords in the GNOME keyring.
- **At home on your desktop.** Written in Rust with GTK 4 and libadwaita, Lookout starts quickly, respects your theme and keyboard, and is gentle on your battery.
- **Private by default.** Your mail renders in a sandboxed view, remote images stay blocked until you allow them, and you decide which senders to trust.

## Mail that keeps up with you

Your inbox should feel like a place you can think, not a pile you keep rearranging.

- **Every account, live.** Multi-account IMAP sync keeps folders current in real time, new mail appears where you are looking, and the unified **All Inboxes** view brings everything into one list when you want the whole picture.
- **Conversations that read like conversations.** Replies collect under a single thread, and the reading pane can show the whole discussion as stacked cards — newest first, older messages folded away until you need them.
- **Search that answers quickly.** Full-text search runs from a local index, so results come back in milliseconds. Type what you remember, or narrow things down with `from:`, `to:`, `subject:`, `has:attachment`, `is:unread`, `category:` and `received:`.
- **The everyday actions, close at hand.** Delete, archive, report junk, pin, mark read, snooze, categorize and flag — on one message or a whole selection. Pinned mail floats to its own section at the top; snoozed mail waits in **Scheduled** until you are ready for it again.
- **Follow-ups with a real date.** Flag a message for today, tomorrow, next week or any date. Overdue flags turn red, group by due date, and show up in Tasks. On Microsoft 365 they stay in step with Outlook.
- **A safety net for send.** Undo a send for a few seconds and your draft comes straight back. Or schedule it for tomorrow morning and let it wait until the right moment.
- **Less noise, more signal.** Categories use Outlook's own colour palette, a Favorites section keeps the folders you live in close, and **Mentions me** finds the messages that actually name you.
- **Junk that learns.** Report junk or not-junk with the right mailbox keywords, block a sender or a whole domain, and let Lookout keep unwanted mail out of your way.
- **Know what is genuine.** Lookout checks sender authentication, flags mismatched display names, can tag mail from outside your organisation, shows where a link really goes before you open it, and can report phishing in one action.
- **Small things that save minutes.** Drag messages onto folders or categories, save every attachment at once as files or one zip, open Outlook's `winmail.dat` attachments like any other file, and get a gentle reminder if you mention an attachment and forget to add it.
- **Read it your way.** Open a message in its own window, inspect its full headers and source, print it, export the raw `.eml`, or reopen a draft straight in the composer where you left off. One-click unsubscribe and read receipts keep the small chores small.

## Writing mail that feels like you

- **A composer that keeps up.** Rich formatting, tables, links and inline images, with a plain-text copy for recipients on older clients. Reply and forward put your cursor right above the quoted message, ready for you to type.
- **The tools where your hands already are.** Compose in the reading pane and the formatting toolbar takes over the main action bar; pop the composer out and the controls come with it.
- **Signatures and templates that fit the moment.** Rich signatures per account, with separate defaults for new mail and replies, plus **My Templates** for the paragraphs you write over and over.
- **Recipients without the fiddling.** Autocomplete from your address books and recent mail, a searchable picker when you want to check people off, and contact lists that expand to their members with one click.
- **Send as you.** Multiple identities per account, with display names, Reply-To and per-identity blind copies — useful for work accounts, personal aliases and mailing-list personas.
- **Say it the right way.** High or low importance, a sensitivity label with a matching banner on the receiving end, a read-receipt request, and spell check that follows your system language or one you choose.
- **Nothing lost.** Drafts autosave while you work, whether the composer is in the reading pane or its own window.
- **Turn a thread into a meeting.** Reply to an email as an event and Lookout opens a new meeting with the thread's participants already invited.

## A calendar you can trust

- **See your schedule your way.** Day, work week, week, month, **List** and **Schedule** views, a split view, a mini-calendar, and an at-a-glance overview on the Mail screen.
- **Schedule at the speed of thought.** Click a slot to start an event, drag to reschedule (hold Ctrl to copy), drag an edge to resize, and right-click for duplicate, copy, categories, show-as and privacy.
- **Your week, your rules.** Choose the first day of the week, your working days and hours, a second time zone in the grid, and even a different time zone for an individual event.
- **Recurrence that behaves.** Edit a whole series, just one occurrence, or this and everything after — and move, edit or delete recurring events individually.
- **Invitations, handled end to end.** Accept, tentatively accept or decline from the email, with a comment, an edited reply, or no reply at all. Propose a new time, forward the meeting to a colleague, and track who has responded in the **Tracking** view. Lookout sends updates and cancellations as things change.
- **A second pair of eyes on your day.** A new invitation shows whether it clashes with your calendar, with a mini day timeline beside it.
- **Find a time that works.** The event editor's **Scheduling** tab pulls free/busy time from your server or Microsoft 365 and suggests times inside your working hours.
- **Join in one click.** Teams, Zoom, Google Meet, Webex and Jitsi links are recognised and offered as a **Join** button in the invitation, the reminder and the event peek.
- **Feeds, files and holidays.** Subscribe to webcal calendars, import and export `.ics`, add a holiday calendar for your country, keep a **Birthdays** calendar built from your contacts, and print your month when paper is easier.
- **Never miss a meeting.** Event alerts arrive as desktop notifications with Open, Snooze and Dismiss — including events that came due while Lookout was closed.

## People, without the busywork

- **Contacts that stay useful.** Create, edit and delete across your CardDAV address books, with anniversaries, IM handles, websites, photos and custom fields.
- **Organised the way you think.** Favourites, contact groups, per-account buckets, a Deleted list that survives restarts, and a **File as** preference that applies globally or per contact.
- **A person, wherever they appear.** Hover or click a name in a message, invitation or contact list to see their details, recent conversations, upcoming shared events and shortcuts to email or meet.
- **Easy to bring along.** Import and export `.vcf` files and Outlook's CSV format, with duplicate detection that updates rather than clutters.
- **One address book everywhere.** Autocomplete in the composer, the invitation field and People all draw on the same contacts — including lists that expand into their members.

## Tasks that actually get done

- **Everything you owe, in one place.** Tasks sync from your calendars and task sources, grouped into Overdue, Today, This week, Later and Completed. The same list appears on the dashboard and beside your mail.
- **Flagged email is a task list too.** Messages you flag for follow-up show up in Tasks; tick one off there and the flag clears in your mail.
- **Built for capture.** One-click completion, a full editor for due dates, priorities, categories and notes, plus **Create task from message** when an email becomes a to-do.
- **A little planning goes a long way.** Pin what you want to tackle today in **My Day**, keep separate lists for different parts of your life, and hide completed tasks when you want the clear picture.
- **Sorted your way.** Sort by due date, importance, title or creation date; group by due bucket, list or category.

## Rules and out-of-office, handled

- **Rules that keep working when the app does not.** Move, copy, delete, flag, categorise, forward or redirect mail — then let each rule run where it makes the most sense: in Lookout, in your Microsoft 365 mailbox, or on a Sieve-capable server.
- **Create a rule from the message in front of you.** Run a folder's rules on demand, and see at a glance where each rule lives.
- **Out of office, without the fuss.** Set automatic replies per account, with an optional date range, and separate internal and external messages on Microsoft 365. A banner in Mail reminds you when they are on, with one click to turn them off.

## Made for Microsoft 365, friendly to everything else

If you use Microsoft 365, opt in to Outlook sync and Lookout brings more of your working life together: a native Graph calendar, contacts and Microsoft To Do lists, plus categories, follow-up flags, rules and automatic replies that stay in step with Outlook. Access is granted explicitly and can be revoked whenever you like.

If you use Google, Nextcloud or your own mail server, Lookout speaks the standards those services already understand. Your mail, categories and flags stay with your account, and rules run on the server where it supports them — not locked inside one app.

## Your day at a glance

- **A dashboard that knows your day.** See who you contact most, when your mail arrives, what is outstanding, and what is coming up — all from local data, updating as you work.
- **An assistant that can look, but not touch.** The optional AI chat works with any OpenAI-compatible endpoint. It answers questions about your mail, people, tasks and events through a small set of read-only tools — it never sends, deletes or changes anything — and its token stays in your keyring.

## Privacy is the default

- **Sandboxed mail.** Messages render in an isolated view, and remote images and content stay blocked until you choose to load them, with per-sender trust you manage one at a time.
- **Suspicious mail, flagged.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC results are checked on arrival, display-name and address mismatches are called out, and outside senders can be labelled.
- **Safe links.** Hover to see where a link really goes; Lookout asks before opening anything whose visible text and real address disagree.
- **Report phishing in one action.** The message is forwarded to the address you choose and moved out of your inbox.
- **Your credentials stay yours.** Passwords and tokens live in the GNOME keyring. Mail, contacts and calendars are cached locally for speed, and never used for anything else.

## At home on your desktop

- **An eye on your inbox, even when the window is closed.** An optional tray icon shows your unread count and offers Open Lookout, Compose… and Quit. Your dock badge can show the same count. Both are yours to switch off.
- **Themes that follow you.** System, Flat Dark or Flat Light, with an accent colour that can match your GNOME scheme.
- **A workspace that feels like yours.** Choose your own background artwork, put the reading pane on the right, at the bottom or out of the way, adjust the spacing, and show up to three lines of message preview.
- **Keyboard first, if you like.** Every action has a shortcut, and every shortcut can be remapped — with conflict checking and a one-click return to defaults.

## Availability

Lookout is packaged for modern Linux desktops — Flatpak, snap, `.deb` and `.rpm` — with a CI pipeline building every release. It is free and open source under the GPL-3.0.
