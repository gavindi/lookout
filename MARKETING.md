# Lookout

**A modern, familiar email client for GNOME.**

Email hasn't gone away — the way we handle it has moved on. We juggle personal
inboxes and work accounts side by side, we live in our calendars, we bounce
between contacts and tasks without thinking. But the mail clients on Linux
still feel stuck in the last decade. Lookout closes that gap: a native GNOME
client that brings the familiar, powerful Outlook-style experience to your
desktop — built for how we actually consume email in our personal and business
lives.

Every account, every message, every meeting, every contact and task in one
place, presented the way you already know. Fast, native, private, and at home
on GNOME.

## Why Lookout

- **Familiar by design.** Three panes, folders on the left, message list in the
  middle, reading pane on the right. A ribbon-style toolbar for the actions you
  reach for constantly. If you've used Outlook — at work, in a previous life —
  you already know how to use Lookout. Nothing to relearn.
- **Native GNOME, through and through.** Written in Rust on GTK 4 and
  libadwaita, Lookout looks, feels, and behaves like part of your desktop —
  because it is. Fast to start, fast to use, gentle on your battery.
- **Zero sign-in friction.** Accounts come from **GNOME Online Accounts**: add
  a Google, Microsoft 365, or Nextcloud account in your system Settings and
  Lookout picks it up automatically. It never touches your passwords and never
  asks you to sign in twice.
- **Or add any account yourself.** Running your own mail server, or with a
  provider GNOME Online Accounts doesn't know about? Add it directly in
  Lookout with your IMAP/SMTP settings — your password goes straight into the
  GNOME keyring, never anywhere else.
- **One workspace, not five apps.** Mail, calendar, contacts, and tasks are
  one application with one consistent interface. Your meeting invite is right
  in the email. Your contact's number is one click away. Your tasks are
  visible next to your schedule.
- **Instant, even offline.** Everything is cached locally. Folders you've
  visited paint instantly, messages you've read open instantly, and your
  contacts and calendar are there even when your connection isn't.
- **Private by default.** Messages render in a sandboxed web view, remote
  images are blocked unless you say otherwise, and you can trust individual
  senders on a per-sender basis. Your mail is your business.

## Mail

- **All your accounts, live.** Multi-account IMAP sync keeps every folder
  current in real time — new mail appears without you asking.
- **Conversations.** Replies group under collapsible thread headers, so a
  busy discussion reads as one conversation, not a wall of messages.
- **Instant search.** Full-text search across every account and folder,
  answered from a local index in milliseconds.
- **The full action set.** Delete to Trash, archive, report as junk, flag,
  mark read or unread, and snooze — applied to one message or a whole
  selection at once.
- **Stay organized your way.** Color tags that follow your mail across
  devices, a Favorites section for the folders you live in, smart per-role
  folder icons, and date-grouped lists that make old mail findable.
- **Drag and drop.** Move messages onto any folder, drop them onto a tag to
  categorize, or drag them right out of the window to save as `.eml`.
- **Polite, practical touches.** One-click unsubscribe from mailing lists,
  request and send read receipts, print any message, and export raw `.eml`
  files on demand.

## Compose

- **A composer that keeps up with you.** Rich HTML editing with inline
  images, formatted text, tables, and links — and a plain-text copy is always
  sent alongside for recipients on older clients.
- **Recipients as chips.** To, Cc, and Bcc as removable chips, with
  autocomplete from your address books and recent mail history.
- **Pop it out.** Pop the composer into its own window and keep working,
  then pop it back in — drafts autosave the whole time, so nothing is ever
  lost.
- **Send as you.** Multiple identities per account, with display names,
  Reply-To, and per-identity blind copies — perfect for work accounts,
  personal aliases, and mailing-list personas.

## Calendar

- **See your week your way.** Month, week, day, and agenda views, a
  mini-calendar sidebar, and an at-a-glance overview right on the Mail screen.
- **Schedule at the speed of thought.** Click any slot to start a new event,
  drag events to reschedule, drag an event's edge to resize it.
- **Recurrence done right.** Edit a whole series, just this occurrence, or
  this and everything after — recurring events can be moved, edited, and
  deleted individually.
- **Invitations, handled.** Accept, maybe, or decline right from the email —
  Lookout replies to the organizer and updates your calendar for you.
- **Feeds and files.** Subscribe to webcal calendars, import `.ics` files,
  and enjoy a synthesized **Birthdays** calendar built from your contacts.
- **Never miss a meeting.** Event alerts arrive as desktop notifications with
  Open, Snooze, and Dismiss actions — even for events that came due while the
  app was closed.

## People

- **Your contacts, fully manageable.** Create, edit, and delete contacts
  across all your CardDAV address books — synced, cached, and always current.
- **Organized.** Favorites, contact groups, per-account buckets, and a Deleted
  list that survives restarts.
- **Portable.** Import and export `.vcf` files, with duplicate detection that
  updates instead of cluttering.
- **Everywhere you need them.** Contact autocomplete powers the composer,
  event invitations, and the People tab itself — one shared address book
  across the whole app.

## Tasks

- **Everything you owe the world, in one list.** Tasks sync from every
  calendar account, grouped into Overdue, Today, This week, Later, and
  Completed.
- **Built for capture.** One-click completion, a full editor for details,
  due dates, and priorities — and Google Tasks support for those accounts.

## The Lookout dashboard

- **Your day at a glance.** A dedicated dashboard shows who you contact most,
  your email volume by time of day, outstanding tasks, and upcoming events —
  all live from your local data, updating as you work.

## Make it yours

- **Themes.** System, Flat Dark, or Flat Light — or let Lookout match your
  GNOME color scheme with a custom accent color.
- **Your background.** Choose your own window background artwork.
- **Your shortcuts.** Every action has a keyboard shortcut, and every
  shortcut is remappable — click to record a new key, with conflict checking
  and one-click restore to defaults.

## Availability

Lookout is packaged for modern Linux desktops — Flatpak, snap, `.deb`, and
`.rpm` — with a CI pipeline that builds every release. Free and open source,
under the GPL-3.0.
